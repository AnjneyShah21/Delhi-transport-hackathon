# Demo script (3 minutes)

1. Open Delhi Ki Public and enter a journey. Explain the relative crowd estimate and 3–4% ticket coverage.
2. Move the comfort slider and compare the direct crowded bus with a less crowded bus/metro option; review time, transfers, walking and seat chance.
3. Show the route segment strip, leave-later tip, and crowd-by-hour pattern.
4. Open heatmap and DTC Insights, export its CSV, and show the usual-trip alert preview.
5. Open `/docs` to show the JSON API. State clearly that current scores are synthetic until source files are loaded and validated.
